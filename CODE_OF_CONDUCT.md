# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
in short form.

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique code, methods and claims, not people.
- Assume good faith; ask before assuming a mistake was careless.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Do not publish others' private information.
- Scientific honesty is part of conduct here: report negative results, state limitations, and do
  not present a result beyond its evidence.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy and may restrict participation
for repeated or serious violations. Report a concern privately to the maintainer through the
contact on their GitHub profile (<https://github.com/soyebmohammad03-dev>).
